About an article

Contain. doesn't write or moderate articles; the SCP Foundation wiki's staff do. To report an article, open it in Contain. and choose Report: in the ••• menu on iPhone and iPad, or Article › Report on the Mac. That hides it for you and opens an email to the wiki's staff.

For copyright or licensing questions about an article, contact the wiki's Licensing team at licensing@scpwiki.com.

Common questions

Why is the first download so large? Contain. stores the whole wiki on your device, about 200 MB to download and about 1 GB once installed, so every article reads offline. Contain. tells you the size and asks before it starts.

Some articles look different. Most articles are drawn natively. A few rely on custom styling and are shown in a web view instead, so they look the way their authors designed them.

Images are missing. Images load from the wiki, so they need a connection the first time you see them.
