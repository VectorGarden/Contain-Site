The SCP Foundation wiki, on your device.

Contain. is a reader for the SCP Foundation wiki: the whole wiki stored on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, readable offline, searchable, and readable aloud.

What it does

  • Offline. The wiki downloads once and stays on your device. Small updates keep it current.
  • Made for reading. Articles are drawn natively, so text follows your preferred size and looks right in light and dark mode. Object classes and ratings sit at the top of each article.
  • Search everything. The full text of every article, offline.
  • Reading orders. Series and canons, in the order they are meant to be read.
  • Listen. Articles read aloud, with redacted text spoken as redacted.

Fiction. The SCP Foundation is a collaborative fiction project. Its articles are horror fiction written by thousands of authors, and some of them are graphic.

Unofficial. Contain. is not affiliated with or endorsed by the SCP Foundation wiki or its staff.
